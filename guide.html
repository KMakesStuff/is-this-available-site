How to use the app

Try it step by step

Start as a guest and follow the timeline through codes, dibs, account creation, listing, sharing and handoff.

Open the interactive demo

Everything in the demo is fictional. No real account or request is created.

View someone’s items

Ask for their share code. Open Settings → Viewing, enter the code and tap Add. Their shared items appear in Browse.

Call dibs

In Browse, select items and review your selection. Tap Call dibs to send the request. Check My dibs for approval and handoff updates.

Share your items

Open My listings to create collections and add items. In Settings → Sharing, create a code and choose the collections it covers. Give the code to people you want to share with.

Review requests

Open Requests to approve or decline dibs. Select approved items to package them, then mark the package delivered after handoff.

Guest or account?

Guest access stays with this installation. Clearing app data or reinstalling may lose it. Create a new account from your guest profile to keep it across devices. Signing into a separate existing account does not transfer guest requests.

Recover your account

Use Forgot password in Settings → Account. Check your spam folder and use the newest reset email.

Refresh

Pull down on the page to refresh your items and requests.
