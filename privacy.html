Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026. This policy describes Is This Available?, operated by Keri Johnson.

Information used by the app

Is This Available? stores account identifiers and, when you create an account, your email address and authentication information. Guest use also creates a backend identity. The app stores names you choose, item titles and descriptions, photos you upload, collection memberships, share codes and saved viewing access, item requests, and package and delivery history.

How information is used

We use this information to run the app: sign-in, account recovery, sharing, requests and handoffs. Your device also stores sign-in sessions, preferences, selections and cached names.

Sharing with other users

People with applicable sharing access can view the items covered by that access. Listing owners can see requests and requester names. Requesters can see the owner identity shown by the app, which may be a display name or email address. Treat share codes as access links and share them only with intended recipients. Someone who sees your information can copy it outside the app; revoking a code does not erase copies they already made.

Service providers

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database storage and uploaded photos. Hosting providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure their services. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See Supabase’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Photos

Only photos you select for listings are uploaded. They are shown to people with sharing access. Avoid putting sensitive information in photos or descriptions.

Retention and deletion

Account information, listings, sharing access and request or delivery history are retained to provide the service until removed through the app or account deletion. In Settings → Account, registered users can choose Delete account, and guests can choose Delete guest data. Account deletion is designed to remove owned uploaded photos and account-linked records and to release outstanding requests on other people’s items. Removing a viewing code is not account deletion. Infrastructure logs and backups may have separate retention periods; they are managed according to the hosting providers’ backup, security and operational practices. Deletion from the active service does not necessarily immediately remove every backup copy.

Children

The app is intended for adults arranging item sharing. It is not designed for children to create accounts or submit personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information.

Policy updates

This page will be updated when the app’s privacy practices change. The effective date identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or help with deletion, contact KeriMakesStuff@gmail.com.
