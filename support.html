App support

For help with Is This Available?, email KeriMakesStuff@gmail.com.

Refresh your items

Pull down on the main page to refresh. If an item has already changed, refresh before trying the action again.

Viewing shared items

Open Settings → Viewing and enter the owner’s share code. The owner controls which collections the code covers and can disable access.

Guest access

Guest access is tied to this installation. Clearing app data or reinstalling may lose access. Create a new account from your guest profile to keep it across devices. Signing into a separate existing account does not transfer guest requests.

Account recovery

Use the password-reset option in Settings → Account. Check spam folders and use the newest reset email.

Delete your data

Open Settings → Account and choose Delete account or Delete guest data. Deletion requires access to the account or guest profile.

When reporting an issue

Include your phone model, app version, what you tapped and what happened. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or recovery links. Redact private information from screenshots.
